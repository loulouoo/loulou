**Title:** The Shortest War in History Lasted 38 Minutes

**Description:**

It started at 9:02 in the morning. It was over before 9:45.

On 27 August 1896, a 29-year-old named Khalid bin Barghash seized the palace of Zanzibar and declared himself sultan without Britain's approval. Five British warships lined up in the harbour, a few hundred metres from a palace made largely of wood. Khalid didn't believe they would fire. He was wrong by about two minutes past nine.

In this case file we break down how a war can be over in under an hour: who started it, who won, what happened to the man who lost, and why the losing side was handed the bill for the shells fired at them.
