**Title:** 5 Unluckiest (and Luckiest) People in History

**Description:**

A woman napping on her sofa is hit by a rock from outer space. A park ranger is struck by lightning seven times. A nurse is on board three famous ships that crash or sink. All of them are real. Every one of them is either the unluckiest person who ever lived, or the luckiest. Maybe both. Today we count down five people who should not have survived, but did.
