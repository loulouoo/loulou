# Abandoned buildings: edit notes

Same edit as the forest and Ring videos: VHS PLAY card, narrated setup over a still, the clip plays out with its own audio, then a slowed and zoomed breakdown. Voice: Holden (ElevenLabs v4). Narration lines are in vo.json, clip ranges in make_spec.py.

Used 8 of the 10 candidates (#4 Georgia woods house and #7 TeEnZiE hospital were never uploaded).

Swearing in the played ranges is bleeped (1 kHz tone): Uncharted Expeditions 1, The Proper People 2, Joe Weller 10, URBEX HILL 3, Sam and Colby asylum 4.

Facts in the narration come from each video's own speech and on-screen captions. Claims the creators make (the "blood" in the motel, over 100 deaths at Forest Haven) are attributed to them.
