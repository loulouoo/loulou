# YouTube description: The Ice Age Diet

Chapter times match the slideshow, with the three recorded parts played back to back (Part 2 starts at 4:30, Part 3 at 9:36). Adjust them if the final edit differs.

---

30,000 years ago, you're sitting by a fire in the middle of an Ice Age winter. Snow up to your knees, wind cutting through your furs, and you're starving. Everyone has told you what happens next: you spear a woolly mammoth and tear into a giant steak.

Except that's not what was on the menu.

In this video we dig through the trash our ancestors left behind and find something much stranger:
- Bread baked 4,000 years before farming.
- Deer legs stored like the first canned food.
- A Neanderthal "fat factory" on a German lakeshore.
- Neanderthals in Spain eating mushrooms, pine nuts and moss.

Then we look at the three tricks that made the human diet different from every other animal's: cooking, grinding wild plants into flour, and passing knowledge down from grandparents to children.

By the end you'll see why the "caveman diet" sold today is mostly made up, and why the sandwich you ate for lunch is a recipe older than farming itself.
