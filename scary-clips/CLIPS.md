# Scary video: "Places Where People Vanished"

A mix of real unsolved disappearances and creepy abandoned places. Every clip below is real footage or a real
photo, not AI. Licences were checked on 2026-09-28 against the Wikimedia Commons API unless noted.

## Licence key
- **Public domain**: use freely, no credit required (a credit is still good practice).
- **CC BY**: free to use and monetize; you **must credit** the author and licence in your description.
- **CC BY-SA**: same as CC BY, and you should also note that your video's use of it is shared under the same licence.
- **Pexels / Mixkit**: free for commercial use and no credit needed (only an individual person being identifiable in a clip needs care).

## Avoid
- Re-uploading viral "caught on camera" ghost clips, TikToks or other YouTubers' urbex videos without a licence (copyright strikes and demonetization).
- The Internet Archive "youtube-…" and "twitter-…" items: they are mirrors of copyrighted uploads, not free footage.
