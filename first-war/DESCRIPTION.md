**Title:** The First War in Human History (13,400 Years Ago)

**Description:**

13,400 years ago, families slept beside the Nile with no walls, no doors and no guards. Many of them never woke up.

In 1965, archaeologists racing the rising waters of the Aswan High Dam uncovered a cemetery at Jebel Sahaba in Sudan. It held 61 men, women and children. Tiny stone arrow tips were still stuck in their bones. Then, in 2021, a new team re-examined the skeletons and found something even darker: healed wounds. This wasn't one battle. It was raid after raid, for generations, thousands of years before farms, cities or kings.

So what were they fighting over? And what does the oldest war ever found tell us about peace today?
