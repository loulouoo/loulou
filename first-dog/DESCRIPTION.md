# YouTube description: The First Dog Was a Terrible Idea

Chapter times assume the three recorded parts play back to back, the same as the slideshow (Part 2 starts at 5:06, Part 3 at 10:19). Adjust if the final edit differs.

---

30,000 years ago, someone sat by a fire in a frozen world, looked at a pack of wolves in the dark, and threw them a piece of meat. It should have been the worst idea in human history. Instead, it gave us the dog.

In this video we dig up the strange graves that prove how far back this bond goes: a sick puppy someone nursed through the Ice Age, a wolf skull with a bone placed in its mouth, and a 12,000-year-old burial where a person's hand rests on a puppy forever. Then we find out how two of the deadliest hunters on Earth stopped being enemies: the leftover-meat trick, the Soviet fox experiment that turned wild foxes dog-like in one lifetime, the tiny eyebrow muscle behind "puppy eyes", and why a dog can read your pointing finger when a chimp can't.

By the end, you'll see why the dog asleep on your bed might be one reason you exist at all.
